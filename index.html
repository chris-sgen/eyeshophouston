WELCOME!

Eye Shop is a second-generation family private practice. The caring mother-daughter duo and their team has a passion for eyecare and eyewear. With advanced, non-invasive technology and a warm and welcoming environment, you’ll love your thorough eye exam at Eye Shop!

EYE CARE HOUSTON - SERVING FAMILIES SINCE 1976

Eye Shop originated as West Houston Eye, a practice that was established by Dr. Linda Rhodes. Since Dr. Ashley Warstler and Dr. Jessica Wong joined the team in 2009, our practice has grown and is excited for expansion on Memorial Drive. We are excited to share with you our brand new office in 2023!
